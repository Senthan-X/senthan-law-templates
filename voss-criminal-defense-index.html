Know Your Rights

Tap each card. Remember them before you need them.

01

Right to Remain Silent

Tap to Reveal

You're never required to answer questions beyond identifying yourself. Anything you say can be used against you — say nothing until counsel is present.

02

Right to an Attorney

Tap to Reveal

If you can't afford one, one will be appointed. Ask for a lawyer clearly and repeatedly — then stop talking.

03

Right to a Phone Call

Tap to Reveal

You're generally entitled to notify someone of your arrest. Use it to reach an attorney, not to explain your side to police.

04

Right to Refuse a Search

Tap to Reveal

Without a warrant or valid exception, you can decline a search of your person, car, or home. Say so clearly and calmly.
